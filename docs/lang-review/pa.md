## 3. Where I was unsure

1. ਮਾਘੀ and ਤੀਆਂ in brackets in `FESTIVALS_PA`: the engine has Makar Sankranti and Hariyali Teej, not Maghi or Teeyan. Remove the brackets if you think they imply a different observance. I added no explanatory sentence about them in the long texts.
2. Months (ਚੇਤ, ਵਿਸਾਖ…) are used for the engine's lunar (amanta) months. Punjabi desi months are solar and begin at sankranti, so the same word can mean a slightly different span.
3. `ਮੱਧਮ` for "neutral" Choghadiya quality (could be ਸਾਧਾਰਨ).
4. Sunrise/sunset labels `ਸੂਰਜ ਚੜ੍ਹਨ ਦਾ ਸਮਾਂ` / `ਸੂਰਜ ਛਿਪਣ ਦਾ ਸਮਾਂ` are long in table cells; ਸੂਰਜ ਡੁੱਬਣ is also used in Punjabi.
5. House ordinals in the oblique form (ਦੂਜੇ ਭਾਵ ਵਿੱਚ): the code glues the word in front of "ਭਾਵ", so only the oblique form fits.
6. Naam-milan help text: Gurmukhi examples (ਪ੍ਰਿਯਾ → ਪੀ, ਕ੍ਸ਼ਿਤਿਜ → ਕੀ) and the rule "ਬ read as ਵ, ਸ਼ as retroflex ਸ਼ or ਸ" are written in Gurmukhi although the input is Devanagari/English; a reader may find the retroflex sh wording unclear (Gurmukhi has only one ਸ਼).
7. `ਸਾਵਣ ਪੁੱਤਰਦਾ`, `ਪੋਹ ਪੁੱਤਰਦਾ` Ekadashi names (Punjabi month substituted for Shravana/Pausha); the rest are transliterations.
8. Karana `ਵਿਸ਼ਟੀ (ਭਦਰਾ)`, tara `ਪ੍ਰਤਿਅਰ`, koota `ਵਸ਼ਯ`: rare words, transliterated.
9. Long nakshatra and rashi traits (27 + 12 paragraphs) read as literal-ish translations of the English; a native editor may want to loosen them.
10. English texts that mention Guru Nanak Jayanti (Kartik Purnima paragraph) were translated as is; there are no Gurpurab dates in the engine.

## 4. Conventions chosen

* Script: Gurmukhi only. No Devanagari and no Latin except WhatsApp, UPI, PDF, IST, Divine Astro, Google Pay/PhonePe/Paytm/FamApp/BHIM/UTR, the Claude/Qwen/Gemma model names, and the e-mail placeholder (`name@gmail.com`, in `KEEP_ENGLISH`/`ALLOW_LATIN`).
* Digits: ASCII (0-9). Gregorian months: ਜਨਵਰੀ, ਫ਼ਰਵਰੀ, ਮਾਰਚ, ਅਪ੍ਰੈਲ, ਮਈ, ਜੂਨ, ਜੁਲਾਈ, ਅਗਸਤ, ਸਤੰਬਰ, ਅਕਤੂਬਰ, ਨਵੰਬਰ, ਦਸੰਬਰ. Weekdays: ਐਤਵਾਰ … ਸ਼ਨਿੱਚਰਵਾਰ (short: ਐਤ ਸੋਮ ਮੰਗਲ ਬੁੱਧ ਵੀਰ ਸ਼ੁੱਕਰ ਸ਼ਨੀ).
* Sentence end: danda `।` in all page text and json; the two NOTE_ texts in names_pa.py use a full stop `.` because tests/test_names_i18n.py rejects the danda for pa (lead may add pa and gu to its allowed list). Question marks `?` kept.
* Possessive apostrophe forms: `’ਤੇ` with the right single quote (U+2019).
* Hindu calendar: amanta, as the English text says; purnimanta notes kept where the English has them. Solar `SOLAR_MASA` not filled.
* Register: polite ਤੁਸੀਂ, imperatives in -ੋ (ਵੇਖੋ, ਪੁੱਛੋ, ਚੁਣੋ). No exclamation marks added.
* Lang switch: Hindi helper lines in the English text (`<span lang="hi">`) were dropped; the sub-lines under h1 are plain Punjabi phrases (as bn does).
* Not translated: gurpurab/Nanakshahi dates (not in the engine), Hindi twin text.
