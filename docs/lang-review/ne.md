## 3. Places I was unsure

- `RASHIFAL_TONE_LABEL.easy` "सहज रूपमा चल्नुहोस्" for "Take it easy".
- `rashifal s.desc`: the tone label follows a dash, so grammar depends on the label form.
- Month framing: the engine uses amanta months (अमान्त) but the Nepali patro is mostly purnimanta. English facts were kept; a reviewer may want a note.
- `VRAT_ABOUT.gudi-padwa` "गुडी भन्ने बाँस": the gudi is unfamiliar to many Nepali readers.
- `jivitputrika`, `hal-shashthi`: नहाय–खाय, पसही चामल kept as transliterated regional terms.
- Digit ordinals such as "11 औँ भावमा" in the rashifal `method` text.
- `p.note` "अपवर्तनसहित सूर्यको माथिल्लो किनारा" (refraction-corrected upper limb).
- `km.mangal`, `km.score_p`: "काट" for cancellation, "चल्नेखालको" for workable.
- `fk.read`, `fk.includes`: long and jyotish-heavy (आरूढ पद, सर्वाष्टकवर्ग, जैमिनि चर कारक).
- `fk.title` still ends "अङ्ग्रेजी र हिन्दीमा", mirroring the English "in English & Hindi". That English wording looks out of date now there are more languages; not changed.
- `NAAM_MILAN_TEXT.explainer`: the T/D/N/Th/Dh letter list stays Latin because it describes English spelling input.
- `boy_ph` / `girl_ph` "जस्तै: राम" / "जस्तै: सीता" (the English has "Ram or राम").
- The Hindi `lang="hi"` subtitle spans were replaced by plain Nepali with `lang="ne"`.
- Nakshatra deities: पूषन्, अजैकपाद, अहिर्बुध्न्य, निरृति.
- app json: transliterated Western terms with no Nepali standard (सेक्ट etc.); केन्द्र/पणफर/आपोक्लिम for angular/succedent/cadent; the tagline "स्विस एफेमेरिसको शुद्धता, वैदिक विवेक" is literary.
- इस्कोन, स्मार्त, वैष्णव in the Janmashtami note.

## 4. Conventions

- Polite तपाईं; buttons use `-नुहोस्`; sentences end with `।`.
- Latin only for Divine Astro, WhatsApp, UPI, PDF, IST, AM/PM, N/E.
- Nepali festival names go in brackets only where the engine's observance is that festival; no observances were invented.
- Placeholders the Nepali may not use (`{english}`, `{name_hi}`, `{city_hi}`) are dropped.
- Several keys (`today.next`, `hub.desc_today`, `VRAT_RULES.head`/`tithi` ...) carry a leading or trailing space because page code concatenates them; checked against en and kn.
